    Accessibility floors

    No theme may lower these. Try the header controls: text size, colour-vision simulation and reduced motion apply to the whole page.

    • Text ≥ 26 px at 1080p on TV, slider 80–200 %.
    • Contrast 4.5:1 on every backing, including bright scenes; 3:1 for icons and bars.
    • Never colour alone: friend ◆, enemy ▼, objective ■ with letters; rarity with names and pips.
    • Subtitles on at first launch, 86 % scrim, speaker names, up to 64 px.
    • Motion: reduced-motion mode, HUD shake off, no more than 3 flashes per second.
    • Input: full remap, hold or toggle for every hold, no press shorter than 300 ms.
